# Turkish citizenship by investment
## Topic cluster
Eligibility and current investment thresholds; property valuation and title-deed requirements; holding period; application process; dependent family members; costs and risks; common reasons for delays.

## Content controls
All legal thresholds, procedures and eligibility claims require dated official references and qualified review. Do not imply citizenship is guaranteed by buying a property or that a particular listing automatically qualifies.

## Candidate questions
Can a foreigner obtain Turkish citizenship through property? Which property documents are needed? Can multiple properties count? What happens if a valuation is lower than the sale price?

## Linking
Link approved guidance to appropriate service pages and eligible property listings only when eligibility has been independently checked.
