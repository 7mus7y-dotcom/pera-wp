# Property management and letting
## Topics
Rental marketing; tenant screening; tenancy agreements; deposits; repairs; maintenance; building management fees; renewal; exit inspections; landlord reporting.

## Candidate questions
Can an overseas owner rent out an Istanbul apartment? How does tenant screening work? Who handles building dues and repairs? How is a property prepared for resale while occupied?

## Controls
Legal rights and landlord obligations must be verified against current Turkish law; avoid blanket claims about eviction or enforceability of undertakings.
