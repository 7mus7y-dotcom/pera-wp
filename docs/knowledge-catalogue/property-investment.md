# Property investment
## Topics
Resale versus new-build; gross versus net floor area; rental yields and vacancy; transaction costs; title checks; valuation; currency exposure; exit liquidity; neighbourhood supply; tenant-occupied purchases.

## Candidate questions
What is the difference between gross and net square metres in Turkey? How should rental yield be calculated? What are the costs of buying an apartment in Istanbul? How can a buyer assess an off-plan developer?

## Evidence
Use dated market observations, original comparisons and explicit assumptions. Do not present estimated returns as guaranteed outcomes.
