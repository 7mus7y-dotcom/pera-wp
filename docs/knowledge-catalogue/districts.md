# Istanbul district knowledge
## Priority clusters
Kadıköy, Şişli/Bomonti, Beşiktaş, Kağıthane, Zeytinburnu/Topkapı, Küçükçekmece/Halkalı/Atakent, Gaziosmanpaşa/Eyüpsultan, Maltepe, Ümraniye, Ataşehir and Maslak.

## District page template
Location and transport; housing stock; buyer/renter profiles; price evidence with date; investment considerations; local risks; relevant properties; adjacent neighbourhood comparisons.

## Editorial safeguards
Clearly distinguish administrative districts from marketing labels. Do not assume a property marketed as 'Ataşehir' or 'Topkapı' is administratively located there. Use dated, attributable price evidence.
